# PRODUCT.md

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio and blog for Kristian Elset Boe, a Norwegian engineer and entrepreneur. The site showcases his work, ventures (Homi, SwipeStats, Boe Ventures), speaking, and travel. It serves as his digital identity and professional home on the web.

register: brand

## Users

- **Potential collaborators, investors, and partners** who want to understand Kristian's background, skills, and current projects.
- **Fellow engineers and builders** exploring his blog posts and technical writing.
- **Recruiters and hiring managers** evaluating his professional profile.

## Brand Voice

Confident, technical, warm. The tone is a builder who ships products, not a corporate consultant. Direct without being cold. Ambitious without being pretentious.

## Visual Identity

- **Theme**: Dark (bg-zinc-950). A builder working late, focused, energized.
- **Accent**: Cyan/teal (Huly.io-inspired). Electric, alive, not neon-garish.
- **Effects**: Huly-style visual effects: dramatic light beams, cursor-following spotlights, electric card borders, glowing buttons. These are signature elements, not decoration.
- **Typography**: System defaults currently. Bold headings, readable body.

## Anti-References

- Generic SaaS landing pages with gradient hero metrics
- Boring developer portfolios with plain text and no personality
- Over-the-top glassmorphism card stacks
- Template-looking portfolio sites with identical card grids

## Strategic Principles

1. **Effects are the brand**: The Huly-style beam, spotlight, and electric borders should be unmissable, not subtle. They define the visual identity.
2. **Dark and dramatic**: The dark theme is a deliberate choice. Effects must read clearly against bg-zinc-950.
3. **Builder energy**: Everything should feel like it was built by someone who loves building.
4. **Content-first with flair**: The content (bio, ventures, blog) is the point. Effects enhance, never obscure.
