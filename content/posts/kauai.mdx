I spent four days on Kauai in July 2025. The photos start with a welcome sign
near the east coast and end with an airplane window on the way out. Between
them are sea turtles, a red-dirt hike, the Wrangler, and far more photos of me
standing in front of a view than any trip strictly requires.

The photos cover July 6 to July 10. That is not much time for an island with
this many roads that end at the ocean, but it was enough to get from the beaches
in the east to Waimea Canyon and the Na Pali overlooks in the west.

## The Jeep

The Jeep Wrangler became the visual thread of the trip. It appears in the
driveway, on the road and again near the overlooks. I am apparently a person
who takes portraits of cars while travelling.

The driveway was wet and surrounded by palms and flowering shrubs. The Google
Maps screenshot is the least romantic image in the set, but it is useful
evidence. The route was about 22 kilometres, and much of the trip was spent
moving between the green interior and the coast.

Later, the road ran straight toward the Pacific with dry golden fields on both
sides. Kauai changes quickly. Dense jungle gives way to open land, and then the
ocean appears at the end of the road.

## Sea turtles

The first day ended at a beach where a crowd had gathered near the water. From
a distance, it looked like a normal busy beach. Closer to the shoreline, the
reason for the crowd was obvious.

  images={[

Several green sea turtles were resting on the sand beside a dark volcanic rock.
One had its head raised. The rest looked completely unconcerned by all the
people and phones around them. Fair enough.

The next day moved inland. The trail was red dirt under a tunnel of trees, with
almost no sky visible through the canopy. It is one of the few landscape photos
in the edit with no people in it. It is better for that. The path simply
disappears into the green.

At the waterfall, I did the arms-out pose on the flat rock above the water. This
would not be the last time on the trip. The trail continued beside a rocky
stream. Other hikers appear in the background, but the water and forest fill
most of the frame.

I still need to confirm the name of this trail before publishing the post.

<ParallaxHero image="/blog/kauai/IMG-0182.jpg" title="WAIMEA CANYON" />

Two days later, the photos move to the west side of Kauai. Waimea Canyon is all
red walls, deep cuts and green ridges. It looks almost impossible to fit into
one image.

The same afternoon continued toward the Na Pali Coast. The Wrangler appears

From the first overlook, the green folds of the coast drop directly into the
Pacific. The colour contrast looks edited even when it is not.

Kalalau Valley is the strongest pure landscape in the set. Deep green ridges
run down toward the ocean, with the valley floor far below.

The arms came out again. I like the walking photo because it adds some movement
between the static portraits.

Earlier in the trip, one evening ended at Tunnels Beach on Kauai's North Shore.
The cliffs sit behind the sand and the last light catches the water. I came away
with far too many versions of the same portrait.

enough. Then I stood for another. That one is quieter, with the cliffs and sea
stacks in silhouette behind me.
