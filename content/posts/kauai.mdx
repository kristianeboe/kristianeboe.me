I spent four days on Kauai in July 2025. The photos start with a welcome sign
near the east coast and end with an airplane window on the way out. Between
them are sea turtles, a red-dirt hike, the Wrangler, and far more photos of me
standing in front of a view than any trip strictly requires.

The photos cover July 6 to July 10. That is not much time for an island with
this many roads that end at the ocean, but it was enough to get from the beaches
in the east to Waimea Canyon and the Na Pali overlooks in the west.

## The Jeep

The Jeep Wrangler became the visual thread of the trip. It appears in the
driveway, on the road and again near the overlooks. I am apparently a person
who takes portraits of cars while travelling.

The driveway was wet and surrounded by palms and flowering shrubs. The Google
Maps screenshot is the least romantic image in the set, but it is useful
evidence. The route was about 22 kilometres, and much of the trip was spent
moving between the green interior and the coast.

Later, the road ran straight toward the Pacific with dry golden fields on both
sides. Kauai changes quickly. Dense jungle gives way to open land, and then the
ocean appears at the end of the road.

## Sea turtles at Poipu Beach

The first day ended at Poipu Beach, where a crowd had gathered near the water.
From a distance, it looked like a normal busy beach. Closer to the shoreline, the
reason for the crowd was obvious.

almost no sky visible through the canopy. It is one of the few landscape photos
in the edit with no people in it. It is better for that. The path simply
disappears into the green.

## On the way to Hanalei Bay

Anahola Beach Park was a nice quick beach stop on the way to Hanalei Bay.

I also loved Foodland Princeville for a poke bowl from the fresh food aisle.
It was much cheaper and nicer than many of the restaurant meals.
