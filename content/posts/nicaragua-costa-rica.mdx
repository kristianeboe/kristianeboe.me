In autumn 2024, I spent about two months in Nicaragua and Costa Rica. First
San Juan del Sur, then Santa Teresa. I stayed at Outsite the whole time,
worked, explored, made some good friends, and went surfing in both places.

Most days were ordinary workdays with a better view. Around them came beach
runs, sunset horse rides, and friendships that followed me home. I also gave
surfing one last proper try. The photos make that part look more successful
than it was.

<ParallaxHero
  image="/blog/nicaragua-costa-rica/san-juan-bay.jpg"

  variant="inset"

Nicaragua came first. San Juan del Sur was my base, with work taking up most
of the days and exploring and meeting people around it. Surfing was part of
the trip here too, though Costa Rica would be where I gave it the most
serious try.

The people were lovely too. On my birthday, someone lured me back from
the gym, and I walked into the kitchen to find everyone waiting with a
cake. I'd only come back expecting the usual post-gym hunger, so this was
a considerable upgrade. Being far from home felt a little less far that day.

From Nicaragua I went to Santa Teresa in Costa Rica. This was the next
chapter of the trip: beach runs, new friends, sunset rides, and another
attempt at becoming a surfer. Plenty to fit around a normal working week.

I stayed at both Outsite locations, which gave me two different versions
of life here: the view from up the hill at Maramar, and the more social
atmosphere at Playa. Between them, I found a routine I could happily have
kept going for a while.

<InfoBox title="Getting from San Juan del Sur to Santa Teresa">

Flying is an option, but I'd recommend arranging a private driver. It's
easy, and you get to see a lot of both countries along the way. Just
remember that you cross the border on foot. With tonnes of luggage,
that part can get sweaty 😅

</InfoBox>

<ParallaxHero

Across the two countries, I was mostly working. The deadlines came with
me, unfortunately. They apparently don't recognise a change of scenery.

Still, I liked having somewhere beautiful to come back to between tasks.
It made an ordinary working week feel like part of the trip, rather than
something I had to get through before the trip could start.

It is up the hill, so I would definitely rent a quad if staying there.
[Playa](https://www.outsite.co/locations/santa-teresa-playa) felt more social.
I liked getting to try both.

On a hike down at Montezuma Falls, near Santa Teresa, we took off our shoes
to cross the river. On the other side, I figured I would take ten barefoot
steps to dry my feet a little before putting my shoes back on.

What could possibly go wrong in ten steps?

On step nine, a caterpillar fell onto my pinky toe. It hurt like hell.

important organ as it could get. Luckily, everything turned out fine.
